# Hollow Knight artwork

The 5 wallpapers are art inspired by Hollow Knight and Hollow Knight: Silksong, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Team Cherry. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-hollow-knight.jpg`: The Knight among lumaflies; artist unknown
- `02-silksong.jpg`: Hornet, Silksong; artist unknown
- `03-mask.jpg`: The Knight's mask; artist unknown
- `04-the-vessels.jpg`: The vessels; artist unknown
- `05-grimm-troupe.jpg`: The Grimm Troupe; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: Hallownest night background, pale mask text, soul blue accent, Hornet red, Greenpath green, infection orange, blue, lavender and lifeblood teal.
